## "asset_subject"

**Data type:**  
String  

**Value:**  
Examples: "folder", "device target", "specimen", "label".

**Description of metadata field:**  
This defines what the asset is a representation of (e.g., a specimen, a label etc). This helps us diffferentiate between the different asset subjects which are treated and reported on differently. It is a way of categorising the types of assets.For non ct scan pipelines this will be populated during the barcode scanning process (ct-scans are presumably always specimens).

**Populated by whom and when:**  
Running pipeline processes and by the integration server.  

**Updated where and when:**  
Never

**Generated by:**  
HPC pipeline process.

**Maps to in ARS:**  
asset_subject

**Maps to in Specify:**  
Title [in part] and Type/description of attachment (remarks) in Specify attachment record
