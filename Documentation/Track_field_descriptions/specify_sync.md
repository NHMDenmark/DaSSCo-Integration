## "specify_sync"

**Type of field:**  
String  

**Part of which document:**  
Track

**Description:**  
This field shows what the overall status for the sync with Specify is.

**Why do we have this field:**  
To keep track of which assets have synced with Specify or what their status in that regard is.  

**Populated by whom and when:**  
Integration server. When an asset is received. 

**Updated where and when:**  
Whenever an asset is synced or set to sync with Specify or when a new asset is received.
