## "process_time "

**Type of field:**  
Integer  

**Part of which document:**  
Track  

**Description:**  
The amount of time in seconds an asset took from being registered by the integration server to it was synced with Specify.  

**Why do we have this field:**  
For optimization and statistical purposes.  

**Populated by whom and when:**  
Integration server. When asset has been succesfully synced with Specify.  

**Updated where and when:**  
Never
